Focus

Four kinds of work.

This is the practice behind the case studies. It is not a menu of every method in quantitative finance.

Agent harnesses for financial decisioning

Tool-using flows with a guardrail, a verification step, and a fail-closed control before a recommendation can affect an action.

Time-series machine learning

Point-in-time histories, sequence and classification models, and out-of-sample evaluation for financial and operating forecasts.

Production data platforms

Immutable raw feeds, quality checks before publish, one publish door, and structured logs that replace a manual record.

Evaluation and scoreboards

Blind comparison of a fine-tune against its untouched starting weights, walk-forward classification, and yearly backtest scoreboards.

The published studies do not include Greek-aware hedging, factor-risk optimization, VaR, or expected shortfall.
